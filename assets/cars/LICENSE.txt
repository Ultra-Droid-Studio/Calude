SOLARA_Cars.glb
  Based on "Low Poly Cars" by cosmo-art (https://cosmo-art.itch.io/low-poly-cars), supplied by the project owner.
  The pack's licence terms could not be read from the build machine; the project owner confirms they obtained the
  pack and that its terms allow use in this game. Credit: "Cars: Low Poly Cars by cosmo-art".
  Modified for SOLARA (tools/cosmo_cars_convert.py): the palette texture baked into per-colour parts, parts renamed
  with roles (paint, glass, lamps, wheels), axes and origin changed, cars renamed (Bastion, Solano, Vortex, Strada,
  Rossa, Cresta, Brava, Furia, Marlin, Patrulla, Chispa, Mula). No real makes or logos are used.
