SOLARA_Pedestrians.glb
  Based on characters of the Microsoft Rocketbox avatar library (Business_Female_01-04, Business_Male_01-06),
  https://github.com/microsoft/Microsoft-Rocketbox, MIT License (text below).
  Modified for SOLARA (tools/peds_convert.py): skeleton reduced from 80 to 20 bones (finger, toe and face weights
  moved to hand / foot / head), body, head and hair textures packed into one 1024 x 1024 atlas (normal maps dropped),
  axes changed (faces -Z), bones renamed Ped_<Id>_<Bone>.
